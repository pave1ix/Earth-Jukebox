How it works

  1. Pick a track. Each one is a NASA view of Earth changing.
  2. Press Listen. First you hear what each sound means, then the music made from the picture.
  3. Ask or explore. Describe this moment gives a summary in words. Explore by touch lets you move across the map and hear each place.

How it reads the data

    Controls

    Every action has a button. Keyboard shortcuts: 1 to 4 choose a track, Space starts or pauses the sound, D describes what is on screen, K plays the listening key, E turns explore mode on, R reads descriptions aloud, Up and Down change volume, and Escape skips the key or leaves explore mode.

    Using a screen reader? The buttons work as usual. Single-letter shortcuts need focus mode (in Narrator, Caps Lock + Space turns scan mode off). In explore mode the map takes the arrow keys directly, and every place you move to is read out.
